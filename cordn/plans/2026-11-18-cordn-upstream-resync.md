### The sender is the leaf credential's account (library audit, 2026-11-23)

**Decision.** An application message's sender is the account that the sending leaf's MLS BasicCredential names (`CordnCredential`, 64 hex ASCII, `spec/00.md` §6). The AAD still has to be present and is still what the envelope `pubkey` is held to (`groupSync.ts:247`, README item 5), and it must now also equal that credential. A payload whose AAD names anyone else is refused (`Delivery.Undecryptable`, "is not the account its leaf credential names"). The lookup is per epoch: a current-epoch message is checked against the current tree, a former-epoch one against the retained tree of the epoch it was sealed at (`CordnCredential.membersAt`), and the former-epoch one must also still hold that leaf now (divergence 7).

**Spec basis.** `spec/02.md` §5: the envelope `pubkey` "MUST equal the authenticated sender identity derived from the sender's MLS credential", and the identity comes from "the stable public-key identity bound to that sender credential as defined in spec/00.md". `spec/00.md` §6 binds the BasicCredential to the account at KeyPackage publication, and both clients verify that binding on every invite (§9). `multi-device.md` §2: "A user's devices share a single MLS leaf per group", so a second device sends from the account's own leaf. An account holding several leaves (two installs that each joined) holds each under its own pubkey. In every honest case the AAD and the leaf credential agree; only a forgery differs, so the check costs no interop.

**What was wrong.** The sender was read from `authenticated_data` alone. That field is authenticated by the AEAD but written by whoever holds the leaf, so any member could post as any account, member or not, and every author-only rule built on the envelope `pubkey` (edits, deletions, poll votes, RSVPs, calendar cancellations in `CordnAnnotationIndex`/`CordnCalendar`/`CordnPolls`) accepted the forgery as the victim's. The old KDoc's reason for preferring the AAD ("a linked device's leaf is not the account") does not hold under cordn's multi-device model. Tests: `CordnSenderBindingTest` (a member posting as another member, as a non-member, a forged deletion, a forged former-epoch message, a second device on the same leaf, honest messages at both epochs, `open(group, sealed)`). The cordn-web app-event vectors are envelope-level and unaffected.

API: `CordnApplicationMessage.open(decrypted, senderCredential)` is new; `open(group, sealed)` checks the credential itself; `open(decrypted)` is deprecated, because it cannot.

**Upstream note (draft for cordn-web and the reference CLI).**

> cordn-web (`chatGroupMessages.svelte.ts`, `decodeAuthenticatedSender(processed.aad)`) and the CLI (`groupSync.ts:259`) take an application message's sender from MLS `authenticated_data` and compare the envelope `pubkey` to it, but never compare it to the credential of `senderLeafIndex`. The AAD is chosen by the sender, so any member can send a message whose AAD and envelope both name another member (or a stranger), and the receiver shows it as theirs, including edits and deletions, which the author-only checks then accept. `spec/02.md` §5 says the identity is "derived from the sender's MLS credential". Proposed fix: after `processMessage`, look up the sender leaf in the epoch's tree (the retained tree for a former-epoch payload), decode its BasicCredential, and reject the message unless it equals the AAD sender. Multi-device devices share the leaf (`multi-device.md` §2), so honest traffic is unaffected. Suggested spec text for §5: "Receivers MUST reject an application message whose authenticated_data sender differs from the identity of the sender leaf's credential." cordn-kmp 1.7.0 does this; a test vector of a forged AAD from a member's leaf would pin it on both sides.
