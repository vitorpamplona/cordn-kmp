## Suggested order

1. C1 (retention) and C8 (trailing metadata). Both are small, contained in cordn-kmp, and protect against message loss and policy forks.
2. C3, plus hiding 1018/31925 and collapsing calendar revisions (the minimal parts of C4/C5). Kind-set and fold changes in cordn-kmp, plus Amethyst row filtering.
3. C7 (per-message media key). cordn-kmp `ingest` returns the epoch key; Amethyst persists it.
4. C2 (own-commit lifecycle). The biggest piece; design it in `cordn-kmp/cordn/plans/`.
5. P2 features: polls, calendar, custom emoji, link forms, `alt` fallback.
6. When this lands, bump the README's "Built from" commit (`cordn-kmp/cordn/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/vitorpamplona/cordn/README.md`) from `b465df0` to `c07a83f`. Note the `spec/03.md` §8 vs `multi-device.md` retention contradiction as divergence #4, and C8 as a found divergence.
